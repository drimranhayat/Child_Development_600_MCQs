# 1
Which sequence best represents the broad prenatal progression?~Zygote, embryo, fetus~Embryo, fetus, zygote~Fetus, zygote, embryo~Zygote, fetus, embryo~The fertilized cell is the zygote. Embryonic organization is followed by the fetal period, during which growth and functional maturation continue.
What is implantation?~Attachment and embedding in the uterine lining~The first breath after delivery~Fusion of two fetal organs~Separation of the placenta after birth~Implantation establishes the early conceptus within the uterine lining. It is distinct from fertilization and from events surrounding delivery.
Why is the embryonic period described as highly sensitive?~Major body structures are being organized~Every organ is already fully mature~Environmental exposures cannot reach the embryo~No cells are dividing~Developing structures can be disrupted during their formation. The consequences of an exposure depend on timing and other factors, not just its name.
Which germ layer is associated with formation of the nervous system?~Ectoderm~Endoderm~Mesoderm alone~The amniotic fluid~The neural plate develops from ectoderm. Germ layers provide an early framework for understanding the origins of different tissues.
Which structures are associated particularly with mesodermal development?~Muscles and much of the skeleton~Only the brain's outer surface~Only the intestinal lining~Only the outer skin layer~Mesoderm contributes to structures including muscle and much of the skeleton. Germ layers differentiate into different tissue systems rather than remaining identical.
How does the fetal period differ broadly from early organ formation?~Growth and maturation of established structures become prominent~All development stops until birth~The fertilized cell first forms~Implantation occurs for the first time~The fetal period includes substantial growth and functional refinement. It does not imply that every organ has completed maturation.
Why should a prenatal week be accompanied by its dating convention?~The same event has different gestational and post-fertilization labels~Week numbers can never describe development~All pregnancies begin on a known fertilization date~Gestational dating starts at the first newborn breath~Dating conventions use different starting points. Specifying the convention prevents a roughly two-week discrepancy from becoming a false biological disagreement.
A fetus shows increasing size after major structures have appeared. Which process is most prominent?~Growth alongside functional maturation~A return to the zygote stage~Completion of all postnatal learning~The disappearance of organ systems~Fetal development includes increasing cell number and size together with maturation. Larger size does not mean every function is ready for independent life.

Why is 'brain structure is complete at birth' too broad?~Organization and maturation continue after birth~The newborn has no brain structures~Brain development starts only at school~All synapses form before implantation~A newborn has an organized nervous system, but its pathways and functions continue changing. What is a dendrite's broad role?~Receiving inputs from other cells~Transporting oxygen through the placenta~Forming the tooth crown~Storing urine in fetal life~Dendrites receive signals that contribute to a neuron's activity. Their development is part of the changing connectivity discussed in the lecture.
Which term is another name for the doll's-eye response listed in the lecture?~Oculocephalic response~Galant response~Plantar grasp~Asymmetric tonic neck response~The doll's-eye response is called the oculocephalic response. Why can reduced oxygen supply matter during prenatal brain development?~Developing neural tissue depends on adequate oxygen~Oxygen has no role before birth~It affects only dental enamel~It always causes the same outcome~Developing tissues need oxygen and nutrients. In the lecture's early nervous-system sequence, what is associated with roughly the third developmental week?~The neural plate~Completed adult myelination~Mature independent air breathing~Completion of all brain connections~The lecture places the neural plate in the early developmental sequence around week three. Prenatal week labels require a clear dating convention; the key relation is early neural organization.

Which inference from prenatal sound exposure is most cautious?~Experience may influence later recognition of familiar sound patterns~The fetus understands every spoken sentence~Language acquisition is complete before birth~All languages are equally familiar at birth~Prenatal exposure can provide experience with sound patterns. A fetus responds to a stimulus with a change in movement or heart rate. What does this show most directly?~Reactivity to stimulation~Mastery of emotional vocabulary~A fixed future temperament diagnosis~Complete cortical maturity~Changes in activity or heart rate can indicate a response. They do not by themselves reveal an exact emotion or predict a whole developmental future.
Which activity does the lecture associate with about 14 developmental weeks as a course anchor?~Breathing and swallowing movements~The first independent steps~Full spoken-word comprehension~Adult-like lung maturity~The slides associate breathing and swallowing motions with this prenatal period. Why can maternal medication be relevant when interpreting fetal behavior?~Some substances can influence activity and development~Every medication has identical effects~Medication can never reach fetal systems~Behavior is always independent of maternal circumstances~Substances differ in their effects and safety. Which behavior appears earliest among these in the lecture's prenatal sequence?~Initial muscle contractions~Eye opening later in pregnancy~A full range of neonatal-type movements~The listed fetal grasp response~The course places early muscle contractions before the later listed grasp and eye-opening milestones. Why should the slide's pregnancy-loss percentage be treated cautiously?~The estimate depends on definitions and the population studied~Every pregnancy-risk percentage is meaningless~It directly predicts one individual's outcome~The percentage applies equally to every clinical situation~Population estimates vary with how pregnancies and losses are counted.
